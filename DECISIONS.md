# Implementation decisions

- **Merged application:** the separate prototype is now integrated into `vikas1188/sitelens-sim`; `public/simulation/` preserves all five physical scenarios, camera controls and training explanations. The prior sibling project remains available separately on the build machine.
- **Real sponsor calls:** RawTree stores complete append-only evidence; Nimble verifies eight curated mappings against official OSHA source text; Jev supplies typed triage. Each event records whether Jev or the deterministic fallback actually made its decision. Noul is a probability, not confidence.
- **Hosted persistence:** private Vercel Blob owns a compact state snapshot and durable outbox, protected by ETag compare-and-set. RawTree owns full event history. Functions await their writes; no filesystem or background timer is required for hosted durability. Reports reject truncated evidence queries.
- **Liquid:** local, keyless LFM2.5-VL-450M via llama.cpp. The hosted camera uses an authenticated outbound local worker and temporary private frame storage. Laptop availability is required for live image inference. Local-mode alert/summary rewriting has a revision guard; hosted narrative uses deterministic templates.
- **State:** five independent zones; at most 12 open-alert references and eight learned rules per zone. Full unresolved counts remain visible. Hosted recent-event cache is capped at 200; historical review hydrates its event from RawTree. Daily counters use UTC and reset on the next observation.
- **Learning:** an exact scoped pattern, human false-alarm verdict, known detector confidence below the learned threshold, and model permission when available are required for suppression. Camera frames do not invent confidence. Simulated learning demonstrates a workflow, not improved image-model accuracy.
- **Human workflow:** acknowledgement is receipt only. Corrective closure requires an action, owner, actual completion timestamp and verifier. False alarms and completed actions remain in evidence. Reopening a correction restores its unresolved count.
- **Reporting:** TXT and genuine DOCX inspection/corrective-action records. OSHA hazard-identification guidance and California sections 3203/1509 guide the record fields; these are not official 300/301 forms. Simulations are excluded by default and explicitly labelled when included. Missing inspection facts remain missing.
- **Vision limitations:** small-model prose can contradict its typed output. Display text is normalized from typed PPE fields; original prose is retained separately. No workers means no PPE violation; hi-viz alone does not become a head-protection alert. Human verification remains necessary.
- **Public demo:** shared hackathon workspace, no user accounts or confidential personnel records. Sponsor/worker credentials stay in ignored local files and server-side Vercel environments. Production and preview have separate evidence tables/state names.
- **Native iOS:** LEAP Swift source is included, but full Xcode signing/linking and physical-device execution are not verified. Browser camera/photo upload and the local model are the supported demonstration path.
